Flickmap – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Flickmap ("the app") is an Android app that shows you how you use your phone: app sessions, app switching, pickups, time-of-day patterns and per-app data use. This policy explains what information the app handles.

In short: everything Flickmap works out stays on your phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and the developer does not collect any of your data.

Usage access

Flickmap only works if you turn on Usage access for it yourself, in your phone's Settings. Before sending you there, the app explains what it reads and why. You can turn usage access off again at any time in Settings (Apps → Special app access → Usage access); the app then stops reading new data.

With usage access, Android lets the app read:

  • App usage events: which app was on screen and when it came to the front or went to the back. Flickmap turns these into sessions, app switches and app-to-app transitions.
  • Screen and lock events: when the screen turned on or off and when the phone was locked or unlocked, which the app counts as pickups.
  • Per-app data use: how much mobile data and Wi-Fi each app used, as Android records it.

The app does not read the content of other apps: no messages, web pages, notifications, photos, typing or anything shown on your screen. It does not read your location, contacts, phone number or call details.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your usage history built from the data above (sessions, switches, pickups, screen-off gaps and data use)
  • The names and icons of the apps that appear in that history, so it can label them
  • The pattern insights it works out from that history (for example late-night use or quick check-ins), which are calculated on your phone
  • Your settings, and whether Flickmap Pro is unlocked

A background task scheduled by Android collects new usage data from time to time, and again when you open the app. It reads from Android on your phone and writes to the app's own database; it does not send anything anywhere.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Backups. Your usage history is left out of Android's cloud backup, so it is not copied to your Google account. Your settings may be included in your device backup to your own Google account if you have Android backup turned on, under Google's terms; the developer has no access to those backups.

The internet

Flickmap does not connect to the internet itself and has no server. The only exception is Google Play: to show the price of Flickmap Pro and to make or restore the purchase, the app talks to the Google Play Store app on your phone through Google's Play Billing Library, which may contact Google under Google's privacy policy. Your usage history is never part of that.

In-app purchase

Flickmap Pro is a one-off in-app purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details under Google's terms; the developer receives no payment details. The app asks Google Play whether Pro has been bought on your Google account and stores only the answer (unlocked or not) on your phone, so it works offline. The developer keeps no record of who bought it beyond the order records Google Play provides.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from the app, it opens your own e-mail app with a message to the developer. You see the message and decide whether to send it; the app sends nothing by itself. Anything you send is used only to reply to you and improve the app.

Permissions

  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): to read the app usage events, screen and lock events and per-app data use described above. You grant it in Settings, and it can be turned off there.
  • Google Play billing (added by Google's Play Billing Library): to buy and restore Flickmap Pro.

The app sees the list of apps that have a home-screen icon, so it can show their names and icons in your history. It does not ask for permission to see all installed apps.

Children

Flickmap is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer using the contact e-mail shown on the app's Google Play listing.
