Iron Interval – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Iron Interval ("the app") is a workout timer for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer does not collect any of your data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Workout sessions and exercises you create or sync
  • Workout history (time per exercise, sets, reps and weights used)
  • Working weights per exercise
  • Your settings, including the Google Sheet link if you add one
  • Cached copies of exercise pictures you have linked

None of this is sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from the app. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your own Google account, under Google's terms; the developer has no access to those backups.

When the app connects to the internet

The app only goes online when you ask it to use content you have linked:

  • Google Sheets sync – when you tap sync, the app downloads the sheet at the link you entered in Settings.
  • Exercise pictures – if your sessions include picture links, the app downloads those pictures and caches them.
  • Video links – opening an exercise's video link hands it to your browser or video app.

These requests go directly from your phone to the sites you linked (for example Google). Like any web request, those sites receive your device's IP address and standard request details, handled under their own privacy policies. The app does not send your workout data, history or any identifiers with these requests.

Sharing and exports

The app only shares data when you choose to: exporting your history as a CSV file, or copying sessions as sheet values, uses Android's share sheet or clipboard, and you decide where it goes.

Permissions

  • Internet – for the sheet, picture and video features above.
  • Notifications and foreground service – to show the current exercise and keep the timer, beeps and voice cues running while the screen is off.
  • Wake lock – to keep timing accurate during a workout.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer using the contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
