Pixsprite – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Pixsprite ("the app") is a pixel art and sprite animation editor for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, it has no internet access of its own, and the developer does not collect any of your data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in its private storage:

  • Your sprites (as .aseprite documents), their thumbnails, and sprites you deleted until the trash is emptied
  • The project gallery, recent files and the reference pictures you added
  • Drive folder workspaces you added: the list of their files, offline copies of those files, and saves waiting to be sent back
  • Your settings (theme, editor and tool preferences)
  • If the app crashes, a short crash report (the error, the app and Android version and the device model), kept until the next launch

None of this is sent to the developer. The app saves your work automatically as you draw (autosave), on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include your sprites and the gallery database (not the settings, thumbnails, trash or Drive offline copies) in your device backup to your own Google account, under Google's terms; the developer has no access to those backups.

Files you open and save

Pixsprite only reads files you pick in Android's file picker or send to it with "Open with", and only writes to files and folders you choose. It keeps access to them so that recent files, reference pictures and Drive folders keep working; removing a workspace gives that access back.

Google Drive folders

If you add a Google Drive folder, the app reads and writes the files you picked through Android's document system and the Google Drive app on your device. When you save to a linked file, or when the app sends saves you made offline once you are back online (using Android's background scheduler, also after the app is closed), the Google Drive app uploads the file to your own Drive under Google's terms. Pixsprite itself does not connect to the internet, and the developer never receives these files. The app only checks whether the device is online, to know when to send waiting saves.

Sharing and exports

The app only shares data when you choose to: exporting a PNG, GIF, sprite sheet or .aseprite file, sharing a sprite, or sharing a crash report uses Android's share sheet or save dialog, and you decide where it goes.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions that need your approval. Its install-time permissions are:

  • View network connections – to know when the device is back online, so saves waiting for Google Drive can be sent.
  • Run at startup and Prevent the device from sleeping – added by Android's background scheduler (WorkManager), so waiting saves are still sent after a restart and can finish while they run.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer using the contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
